# Senior Care --- Product Overview

## Product Vision

Senior Care is a family and professional care coordination platform that
helps seniors remain independent while allowing family members and
professional caregivers to coordinate daily care.

The same product supports four usage modes:

1.  **Solo** --- a person manages their own routines, medications,
    appointments, and care information.
2.  **Family Care** --- family members coordinate care for a senior.
3.  **Professional Caregiver** --- a caregiver manages one or many
    seniors.
4.  **Mixed Care Circle** --- family members and professional caregivers
    work together around one senior.

The product must not require a separate application for professional
caregivers. The role and relationship model determine what the user sees
and can do.

## MVP Objective

Validate this hypothesis:

> Families, seniors, and professional caregivers can reliably coordinate
> daily care using a shared care workspace.

## MVP Core Loop

1.  Create or join a senior's care circle.
2.  Invite family members and/or professional caregivers.
3.  Create recurring care tasks, medication schedules, and appointments.
4.  Assign responsibilities.
5.  Caregiver or senior completes the work.
6.  Record the completion as a care event.
7.  Family/care-circle members see the activity.
8.  Missed or overdue items trigger appropriate reminders/escalation.

## Product Principles

-   Senior-first and independence-preserving.
-   Family coordination rather than surveillance.
-   Professional and family caregivers use the same core system.
-   Solo use must work without another caregiver.
-   Fast, simple, accessible mobile UX.
-   Offline-friendly for core care workflows.
-   Privacy and least-privilege access by default.
-   Medical information is recorded and coordinated, not diagnosed by
    the MVP.
-   Build the smallest useful product before advanced AI, wearables,
    marketplaces, or agency management.
-   Visual identity is calm, trustworthy, warm, modern, and dignified.
-   Primary visual direction is green with a slight blue/teal bias.

## MVP Modes

The MVP supports:

-   Solo self-care.
-   Family care.
-   Professional caregiver.
-   Mixed family + professional care.

A user can start alone and invite other people later.

## Out of Scope for MVP

-   AI diagnosis or medical recommendations.
-   Fall detection.
-   Continuous wearable monitoring.
-   Pharmacy integrations.
-   Insurance integrations.
-   Telemedicine.
-   Caregiver marketplace.
-   Payroll/billing.
-   Full home-care agency management.
-   Complex clinical workflows.
