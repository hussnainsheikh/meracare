# Post-MVP Product Roadmap

## V2 --- Care Management

Goal: turn basic coordination into a complete care-management product.

### Health Tracking

-   Blood pressure
-   Blood glucose
-   Weight
-   Temperature
-   Heart rate
-   Symptoms

### Medical Records

-   Prescriptions
-   Lab reports
-   Documents
-   Medical history

### Medication Improvements

-   Refill reminders
-   Adherence reports
-   Prescription history

### Appointment Improvements

-   Doctor profiles
-   Appointment preparation
-   Follow-up tasks
-   Appointment notes

### Professional Caregiver Improvements

-   Schedule
-   Shifts
-   Availability
-   Work history
-   Care plans
-   Daily/weekly reports

## V3 --- Care Intelligence

Goal: turn collected care data into useful summaries and proactive
attention signals.

### AI Weekly Summary

Summarize:

-   care completion
-   medication adherence
-   health measurements
-   caregiver notes
-   upcoming appointments

### AI Care Assistant

Examples:

-   What do I need to do today?
-   How has Mom been this week?
-   What changed?
-   What did the caregiver report?
-   Prepare me for the appointment.

### Routine Anomaly Detection

Compare current activity to the senior's established routine.

The system should surface deviations, not diagnose.

### Smart Care Reports

Generate human-readable summaries for family and authorized
professionals.

## Product Expansion Rule

Do not implement a V2/V3/V4 feature merely because it is technically
interesting.

A feature should be added because:

1.  MVP users demonstrate the need.
2.  It improves retention or value.
3.  It fits the care coordination model.
4.  It can be implemented without compromising privacy/safety.
